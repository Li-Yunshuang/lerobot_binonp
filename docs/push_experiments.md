## Reading these

- Compare within an eval set and harness. Numbers before and after the settle fix are not
  comparable (the fix alone moved the baseline +2.5 pp primary / +5.4 pp strict).
- Old-stack rows (arms in cloud, per-key MIN_MAX, absolute actions) are a different regime;
  their ablation conclusions do not transfer to the new stack.
- Means and stds are dominated by the failure tail (successes ~15 ± 11 mm, failures ~75 ± 57 mm);
  medians and p90 describe typical precision and tail severity separately.
- `ever-in-goal` needs per-step traces, present only on corrected-harness evaluations.
